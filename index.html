Fishing Companion Archero 2

How to use

  • Log every catch. Tap the fish you landed — the pool shrinks, the odds update, and Undo reverses the last action completely.
  • Pick the multiplier before you draw. The 1× 2× 5× 10× row sets how many fish a draw lands. The batch closes at that many and is recorded as one draw of that size, so the plan's 10× count is exact and 1× weight casts never inflate it. Switching mid-draw retargets the open batch instead of losing it.
  • The band under the gauge tells you whether to break a line now or thin the pool first — odds are legendary ÷ fish left, so thinning is cheaper than spending lines at the 2.5% baseline.
  • Two directives. The method tracker carries both published plans — Option 1 maximises gold tickets for +2 runes on a smaller gem budget, Option 2 maximises silver tickets for +3. Switch at the top of the tracker; only the targets move, never your progress.
  • Most of the tracker fills itself. Legendary catches, 10× draws and golden fish are all counted from what you log, and each ± is there to correct a count, not to keep it. Golden fish are accumulative across every pool, Dragon Gate included.
  • Weight stays manual — every fish weighs something different, so there is nothing to read it from. Those rows are marked manual and never gate a region.
  • Goals announce themselves and then get out of the way. Finishing a step notifies, not just finishing a region; several at once queue rather than overwrite.
  • A Dragon Gate is a random extra region, not a step in the plan. Tap the amber button to fish it; a legendary you pull there credits the region you were working, and Undo takes both back.
  • Broken lines track against the 120 cap.
  • Progress saves itself in this browser. There is no backup — add the page to your Home Screen and it behaves like an app.
