Fishing Companion Archero 2

How to use

  • Tap a fish to log a catch — the pool shrinks and the odds update.
  • The band under the gauge tells you whether to break a line now or thin the pool first.
  • Legendary catches feed the method tracker automatically; you get a nudge when a region's goal lands.
  • Pick the multiplier you're running before you log a draw. The batch closes at that many fish and is recorded as one draw of that size, so the plan's 10× count is exact — 1× and 2× casts never inflate it. Switching mid-draw retargets the open batch instead of losing it.
  • Weight stays manual: each fish weighs something different, so there is nothing to read it from.
  • A Dragon Gate is a random extra region. Tap the amber button to fish it; a legendary you pull there counts toward the region you were working, and Undo takes it back.
  • Track broken lines against the 120 cap.
